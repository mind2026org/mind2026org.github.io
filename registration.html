Registration Now Open

MIND 2026 registration is now open.

Participants are invited to complete registration through the appropriate channel. Domestic participants may register through the domestic portal, while international participants may use the international registration portal. Direct links and QR codes are provided below for convenient access.

For Domestic Participants Domestic Registration Portal
For International Participants International Registration Portal
Registration Fee
Before October 7, 2026
  • 3800 CNY regular registration with 1 paper (A)
  • 3400 CNY student registration with 1 paper (B)
  • 2500 CNY abstract registration
  • 2500 CNY regular attendee with no paper
  • 2100 CNY student with no paper
After October 7, 2026
  • 2900 CNY abstract registration
  • 2900 CNY regular attendee with no paper
  • 2500 CNY student with no paper
Extra paper fee: 1000 CNY for each more paper (A or B)
Registration with accepted paper(s) should be completed by October 7, 2026.
The Registration Fee Includes
  • Keynotes and Technical Sessions (November 14-15, 2026)
  • Tutorials (Friday, November 13, 2026)
  • Coffee Breaks (November 13-15, 2026)
  • Lunch Buffets (November 14-15, 2026)
  • Welcome Reception (Friday, November 13, 2026)
  • Conference Banquet (Saturday, November 14, 2026)
  • Paper Processing Fees (Applicable to A and B)
Registration Policy
1
Author registration: At least one author of each accepted paper must register for the conference no later than October 7, 2026. The registration will benefit from early-bird rates and provide access to special hotel discounts with limited availability on a first-come, first-served basis. If, after having registered, an author or coauthor of a paper is unexpectedly NOT available to present the paper at the conference due to, e.g., a visa issue, please contact icmind2026@gmail.com and we will try to find a solution on a case-by-case basis.
2
Cancellations and refund: Cancellation requests received on or before October 30, 2026, will be subject to a processing fee of 30% of the original registration fee. No refunds will be issued for cancellations made after October 30, 2026. All cancellation and refund requests must be submitted via email to icmind2026@gmail.com and received by the stated deadline.
3
Eating restrictions: During registration, please indicate your dietary restrictions. All food functions will have vegetarian items. We are striving to provide diverse culinary options; however, we may not be able to meet your dietary restrictions. Please contact icmind2026@gmail.com if you have any other questions about registration. All requests for selection items change, after the completion of registrations, must be provided in writing by email to icmind2026@gmail.com.
